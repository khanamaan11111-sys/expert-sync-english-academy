Mastery Gates

Mastery here means the learner reports confidence of 5/5. Practice Ready is 4/5, Developing is 3/5, and Focus Required is 1–2/5. These are coaching thresholds, not formal proficiency certification.

What happens next?

A topic at 5/5 moves to maintenance and mock-interview practice. A topic at 4/5 should receive rapid-fire practice. A topic at 3/5 needs standard-answer and follow-up practice. A topic at 1–2/5 returns to concept review and the Retry Queue.
