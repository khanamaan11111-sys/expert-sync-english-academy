Connection & feature check

Check basic access to app features. This does not send messages or change your data. Slow requests time out after eight seconds. Retry when connected. Passing these checks does not verify every feature or photo permission.

Your profile data

Download your profile name, username, bio and photo path as JSON. This download does not include chat history, posts or uploaded photo files.

Account deletion

A deletion request is sent to the app’s administrator queue. Your account stays active until an administrator processes it. You can cancel while it is pending. Downloaded copies held by others cannot be erased.

Pending deletion requests

Administrator processing required. Verify the current request and account ID before removing any account in Supabase.

Blocked members

Blocking stops new direct messages in both directions. Existing chats, community posts and shared groups remain visible.

My reports

Report a post or message using its Report button. Your reason and the selected content’s text are shared with moderators. Reporting does not automatically remove content.

Moderator review

Latest 100 open reports. Review notes are visible to the reporter. Mark reviewed records a decision. Use Remove reported post or Clear reported message to remove content, with a reason and confirmation. Stored photo files and downloaded copies are not erased by these actions.

How to use Expert Sync

Use Chats to find a friend by name or username. Choose a matching profile to preview it and start chatting. Open Photo feed to publish photos, like posts and add comments. Edit your name, username, bio and photo using Edit profile in Chats.

Photos must be JPEG, PNG or WebP and no larger than 2 MB. Use Ctrl+Enter to send a message on a computer.

On iPhone, use Safari's Share menu and Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Install Expert Sync when available, or your browser's install menu.

Privacy and community rules

Posts and comments are visible to signed-in members. Profile names, usernames, bios and photos can be viewed by other members. Private chats use membership access controls; end-to-end encryption is not implemented.

Be respectful. Do not share passwords, sensitive personal information, abusive content or images you do not have permission to share.

Your login session stays on this device. Sign out on shared devices. The app does not store chats or shared photos for offline use.
