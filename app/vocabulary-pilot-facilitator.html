Pilot Decision Guide

Keep: students understand the feature with little explanation and use it voluntarily.
Improve: students repeatedly hesitate, misinterpret instructions, or abandon a flow.
Investigate: feedback conflicts with observed behavior or the prototype lacks enough evidence.
Do not conclude: a small pilot does not establish CEFR improvement, long-term retention, or population-wide satisfaction.
