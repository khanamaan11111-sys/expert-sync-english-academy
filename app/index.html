⚠️ Important Job Safety Warning

Never pay money to get a job. Be careful of registration fees, interview fees, security deposits, training fees, document-verification fees, offer-letter fees and placement fees.


Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, bank password or sensitive financial information.
