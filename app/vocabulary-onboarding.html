🎯 Recommended First Session

  1. Learn 5–10 words in Dictionary.
  2. Complete Context Mastery.
  3. Write at least 3 original sentences.
  4. Practice pronunciation.
  5. Return for review later.

Important: completion badges and streaks represent activity. They are not official CEFR certification or a substitute for teacher assessment.
