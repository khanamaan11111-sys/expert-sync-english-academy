STAR Framework

S

Situation

Set the context briefly. What was happening?
T

Task

Explain your responsibility or objective.
A

Action

Describe what you did, in clear sequence.
R

Result

State the outcome and what you learned when relevant.

Practice Prompts

Your Answer

Interview Tip

Keep Situation and Task concise. Spend most of your time on Action. Give a concrete Result when possible. Never invent an experience; if a question asks about something you have not experienced, explain what you would do or use a clearly labelled training example.
