Four-Level Ladder

LEVEL 1

Foundation — Understand

Identify the term, concept or process and explain it in simple English. Example task: define PNR or GDS.
LEVEL 2

Recall — Explain

Give a concise interview answer without relying on the screen. Add one useful distinction or practical point.
LEVEL 3

Application — Handle

Apply the knowledge to a customer, reservation, ticketing or operational scenario and explain the reasoning.
LEVEL 4

Advanced — Adapt

Handle follow-up questions, distinguish related concepts, communicate uncertainty honestly and adapt the answer to the interviewer.

Progression Rule

Do not move a learner upward simply because they memorized an answer. A learner should demonstrate understanding at the current level before progressing. Technical procedures must remain aligned with the applicable current airline/GDS/employer workflow.

Practice Path

Foundation → Recall → Application → Advanced → Mock Interview

Use the Question Bank for Foundation/Recall, Rapid Fire for Recall, Scenario Lab for Application, and Mock Interview for Advanced practice.
