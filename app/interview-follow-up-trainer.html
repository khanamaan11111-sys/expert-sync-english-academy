Follow-Up Strategy

Listen → Identify what changed → Answer the new point → Stay concise → Ask for clarification if necessary.

A strong first answer does not guarantee a strong follow-up. Students should practise adapting rather than repeating a memorized script.

Professional rule

Never invent airline, GDS, employer or operational experience. If you do not know a technical detail, acknowledge the limitation and explain how you would verify it through the applicable authorized source or workflow.
